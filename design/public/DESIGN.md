## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the contemplative, tactile dignity of a world-class public reference library brought into modern digital clarity. Merging the curated, warm humanist warmth of independent literary presses with the exacting, open-access taxonomy of public archives, the aesthetic is deliberately intellectual, serene, and uncluttered. 

The visual style blends **Literary Editorial Minimalism** with soft **Tactile Depth**. Heavy archival whitespace, warm paper-tinted surfaces, and book-cover elevations replace commercial e-commerce urgency. The experience avoids transactional distraction—omitting shopping bags, carts, or gated barriers—focusing entirely on serendipitous exploration, bibliographical richness, and reading culture.

## Colors

The palette reproduces physical reading rooms: linen bindings, dark heritage woodwork, and heavyweight rag paper.

- **Primary (`#1A382B`)**: Deep archival forest green anchors the navigational spine, principal buttons, and primary headings. It communicates permanence, civic stewardship, and intellectual calm.
- **Secondary (`#8A6B47`)**: Warm bookcloth buckram acts as an editorial bridge, reserved for curatorial notes, sub-headings, and active state highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#D8CEBE`)**: Pressed parchment beige serves as quiet contextual framing, selected badge treatments, and interactive hover planes.
- **Neutral (`#2C302E`)**: Dark slate ink text that eliminates the harsh fatigue of pure `#000000` while retaining AAA contrast against ivory canvas backgrounds.
- **Surface & Backgrounds**: Base canvas defaults to `surface-cream` (`#FAF8F5`), layered with structured modular sections in `surface-card` (`#F5F2EB`).
- **Borders & Dividers**: `border-subtle` (`#E5DFD5`) establishes gentle structural order without creating visual imprisonment.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional dialogue between longform literary tradition and crisp information retrieval. 

- **Display & Headings**: Rendered in **Newsreader**, an optical serif drawn specifically for immersive reading. It lends dignified editorial authority to author names, book titles, curatorial essays, and collection highlights. Headline styling defaults to regular and medium weights to preserve classical bookcraft proportions.
- **Body & Labels**: Built with **Plus Jakarta Sans**, providing high-legibility geometric clarity for bibliographical metadata, call numbers, availability indicators, and dense index filtering.
- **Numbers & Metadata**: Publication dates, Dewey classifications, and pagination numbers within sans-serif blocks maintain neutral tabular balance to ensure rapid vertical scanning across inventory lists.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a responsive 12-column grid system bound by maximum content widths to prevent excessively long typographic lines.

- **Desktop Layout**: 12 columns with a max container width of `1280px`, a central `gutter` of `1.5rem`, and outer margins of `3rem`. Catalog viewing shifts fluidly between a balanced 4-column book jacket showcase and an analytical 2-column split (metadata on the left, subject trees and excerpt quotes on the right).
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8 columns with `1rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Complex facet sidebars convert into an off-canvas drawer or collapsible horizontal pill clusters.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` outer margins. Book browsing renders in staggered 2-column tiles or single-column bibliographical rows.
- **Rhythm Philosophy**: Generous vertical section spacing (`space-xl`) prevents inventory cognitive overload, encouraging unhurried, library-style pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tactile and architectural rather than digital or neon-lit:

- **Surface Tiers**: Base view sits on `#FAF8F5`. Elevated modules (shelves, reading room panels) utilize `#F5F2EB` and `#FFFFFF`, separated by a hairline 1px stroke of `border-subtle` (`#E5DFD5`).
- **Physical Book Drop Shadows**: Book covers use asymmetric, multi-stop diffusion simulating natural light overhead onto heavy cardstock:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(26, 56, 43, 0.05), 0 10px 24px -4px rgba(26, 56, 43, 0.12), -2px 0 0 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04) inset;`
  The faint negative inner shadow mimics the rounded spine bend of physical volumes.
- **Card & Sheet Elevation**: Discovery cards avoid high-contrast drop shadows. Resting states feature flat backgrounds bounded by crisp `1px` borders. On hover, they gain a gentle warmth:
  `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -6px rgba(44, 48, 46, 0.08); transform: translateY(-2px);`
- **Modal & Search Drawers**: Global catalog search overlays apply an ambient, warm slate backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.85);`).

## Shapes

The geometric vernacular is soft and structured (`roundedness: 1`), evoking cleanly cut heavy deckle-edge paper, index cards, and architectural reading desks.

- **Base Radii (`0.25rem` / `4px`)**: Buttons, text inputs, catalog metadata tags, and shelf category tabs.
- **Cards & Enclosures (`0.5rem` / `8px`)**: Book detail cards, preview modal sheets, and curation highlights.
- **Book Covers (`2px` spine radius, `0px` edge)**: Book images are kept sharp at the outer right edge to maintain the realistic silhouette of trimmed paper block leaves.
- **Circular Elements**: Limited solely to author portrait avatars, patron bookmark icons, and catalog status indicator dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Locate on Shelf", "View Catalog Entry")**: Solid `#1A382B` background, `#FAF8F5` text, subtle `4px` corner radius. Hover state transitions to `#142E23` with no drop shadow.
- **Secondary / Outline ("Save to Reading List", "Cite")**: `1px` border of `#1A382B`, transparent background, `#1A382B` text. Hover fills with `#EFE9E0`.
- **Text Action ("Full Table of Contents →")**: Underlined text in `Newsreader` italic, displaying an animated transition on underline thickness.

### Chips & Subject Tags
- Height: `28px`. Background: `#FAF8F5` with a `1px` border in `#E5DFD5`. Text: `#5C6460` in `label-sm` uppercase.
- Selected / Active: Background `#1A382B`, text `#FAF8F5`, border `#1A382B`.
- Dewey / Call Number Badges: Monospaced numerical prefix, background `#EFE9E0`, text `#1A382B`, borderless.

### Discovery Cards (Book Jackets)
- Comprises an elevated book cover with spine shading, titled in `Newsreader` (`headline-sm`), author line in `Plus Jakarta Sans` regular, and an unobtrusive status pill indicating "On Stacks" or "Archival Reference".
- Action triggers avoid commercial carts; instead they provide "Read Overview", "Explore Subject Tree", and "Locate Physical Copy".

### Catalog List Row View
- Dense, structured bibliographical entry with a left-aligned micro-thumbnail, middle title/author/year stack, subject tags, and right-aligned branch availability locator.
- Separated with thin `1px` bottom borders in `border-subtle`.

### Input Fields & Search
- Generous `48px` or `56px` height for central catalog search.
- Background: `#FFFFFF` encased in a `1px` `#E5DFD5` border. Text uses `#2C302E` with placeholder text in `#5C6460` italic. Focus state transitions border to `#1A382B` with zero outer neon glow.
- Integrated keyboard shortcuts indicator (`⌘K` / `Press / to filter`).

### Selection Controls
- Checkboxes and radio buttons feature precise square/circle geometry using a `1px` `#1A382B` border. Selected states fill with `#1A382B` housing a cream checkmark.

### Curatorial Showcase Feature
- Large banner module showcasing a librarian-curated collection. Features an unhurried layout, an italicized quote, editorial excerpt, and an open deck of overlapping book spines with textured depth.
